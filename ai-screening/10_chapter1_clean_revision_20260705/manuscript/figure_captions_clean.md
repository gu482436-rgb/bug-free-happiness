# Clean Figure Captions

## Figure A

**Figure A. Enzyme-level peptide occurrence counts in the expanded V2 in silico digestion library.** Bars show the sum of unique peptide occurrences contributed by each enzyme rule across the 15 fish collagen input sequences before global deduplication. A peptide generated by multiple enzyme rules can therefore be counted more than once in this panel. These counts should not be interpreted as the final number of globally unique V2 peptides, which was 4,515 after deduplication.

## Figure B

**Figure B. Random Forest and XGBoost prediction landscape for the V2 peptide library.** Gray points represent V2 peptides outside the Top100 selection set (n = 4,415), blue points represent Top100 candidates excluding the algorithmic Top5 (n = 95), and red stars represent the V2 algorithmic Top5 (n = 5). The plot shows model-score distribution only; high prediction probability is not experimental antiviral evidence.

## Figure C

**Figure C. Multi-criteria comparison of the V2 algorithmic Top5 candidate peptides.** All axes are normalized to 0-1. Mean ML probability, AI4AVP score, ToxinPred-derived computational low-risk score, AllerCatPro "no evidence" flag, sequence shortness, source species coverage, and computational VP1 docking score are shown as prioritization features. ToxinPred, AllerCatPro, and docking values are computational indicators only and do not establish safety, non-allergenicity, or viral binding.

## Figure D

**Figure D. External computational validation summary for the V2 Top20 candidate peptides.** The left panel shows continuous computational scores, including mean ML probability, AI4AVP score, and ToxinPred-derived low-risk score. The right panel shows AllerCatPro output as a separate categorical flag, where "no evidence" indicates no matched allergen evidence in that tool and does not mean confirmed non-allergenicity. The figure summarizes prediction-level evidence only.
